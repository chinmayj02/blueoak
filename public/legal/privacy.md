When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

## How We Use Your Information

We use your data only for the following purposes:

- To respond to your inquiry or service request from BlueOak Ventures
- To send you relevant program updates, service information, or event invitations (with your consent)
- To contact you via email, phone call, or WhatsApp for follow-up — by submitting your number, you consent to this

- To run retargeting or custom audience advertising on platforms like Meta (using hashed or anonymised data)
- To comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations

We do **NOT** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their commercial gain.

## Legal Basis for Processing

BlueOak Ventures processes personal data in accordance with applicable Indian laws (Information Technology Act, DPDPA etc.). We rely on your consent (given when you submit a form or interact with our ads) and our legitimate business interest to process your data.
